- (capability) CAP-1 (FR-1): Content editors can set title tag, meta description, and URL slug on every page/post on both sites without developer involvement; success: every published page has a distinct editable title+meta, and slugs stay editable post-publish without breaking internal links.
- (capability) CAP-2 (FR-2): Every page on both sites shows click-to-call, a Zalo entry point, and Google Maps using that site's own contact details; success: present and functional on 100% of published page templates on both sites.
- (capability) CAP-3 (FR-3): A lightweight contact/quote-request form is embeddable on any product/category page on either site; success: every submission is retrievable afterward with all fields intact and is routed per architecture (AD-3).
- (capability) CAP-4 (FR-4): Each site reports to its own GA4 property and is separately verified/submitted in Search Console; success: tracking fires on all page types per site, each site has its own submitted sitemap and visible indexing status from launch.
- (capability) CAP-5 (FR-5): Site A and Site B link to each other only through contextual, editorial links embedded in real content; success: no automated, sitewide, identical link block exists on either site.
- (capability) CAP-6 (FR-6): Each of Site A's 11 confirmed categories gets its own SEO-optimized page; success: each resolves to a unique URL with category-specific title/meta/content and ranks for at least one assigned target keyword within the SM-1 window.
- (capability) CAP-7 (FR-7): Site A has a blog/news section for long-form advice content, separate from the product catalog; success: posts are discoverable from Site A navigation and indexable independent of product pages.
- (capability) CAP-8 (FR-8): A dedicated free-survey-request form exists on Site A's installation-required category/product pages, distinct from the general contact form; success: every submission captures at minimum location/address, product of interest, and callback contact; an out-of-service-area entry gets a non-blocking warning but is never rejected.
- (capability) CAP-9 (FR-9): Each of Site B's 3 product lines (trong unified across all types, thung ruou go, bon tam go) gets its own SEO-optimized page(s); success: each resolves to distinct URLs with line-specific title/meta/content, and priority drum keywords are tracked per SM-1.
- (capability) CAP-10 (FR-10): A craftsman/heritage story page tells the Doi Tam workshop/craftsman story and is linked from all three Site B product-line pages; success: includes photo content at minimum, with video as a should-have.
- (capability) CAP-11, should-have (FR-11): A read-only drum configuration reference (size x loai 1/2/3 x banh xe x son x ve mat trong) ends in a quote request, never a checkout; success: if built, shown configuration options match what sales actually offers; may slip past launch if it competes with the Tet 2027 deadline.
- (capability) CAP-12 (FR-12): Editors can create paid-ads landing pages on Site B, distinct from organic category/product pages and excluded from primary navigation; success: at least one landing page for thung ruou go exists and is live before Tet Am Lich 2027 (~mid-Feb 2027).
- (capability) CAP-13 (FR-13): Landing pages show specific per-variant pricing, unlike Site B's organic product pages which stay contact-for-quote; success: each wine-barrel variant (go soi, ngua keo, 1 ngua, 2 ngua) shows its own price, paired with a 'Dat mua ngay' CTA routing to contact/Zalo/phone, never to a cart or online payment.
- (capability) CAP-14 (FR-14): Site B has a blog/content section covering craft/heritage, wood-care advice, and Tet-gifting topics, following the same pattern as CAP-7; success: own per-page SEO fields, indexable, discoverable from Site B navigation, separate from the product catalog.
- (constraint) One shared Piranha CMS instance and database serves both sites (Site C joins later as a third Site record) — never per-site deployments (architecture-spine AD-1).
- (constraint) Product taxonomy is modeled as Archive+Post (danh muc/blog); standalone content (landing pages, craftsman story page, homepage, category hub) is a standalone Page — no ad-hoc content modeling (AD-2).
- (constraint) Lead-form submissions (CAP-3, CAP-8) write to one shared custom FormSubmission table plus a Piranha Manager extension — never Piranha's built-in Comment/PostComment feature (AD-3).
- (constraint) All redirects, same-domain and cross-domain, route through Piranha's native Alias/AliasRouter — no custom redirect middleware (AD-4); cross-domain aliases into Site B are created only after Site B's URL structure is frozen.
- (constraint) Stack is forced to .NET 8 + Piranha CMS 12.2.0 + MariaDB 10.11 LTS, self-hosted on CentOS Stream 9 — Piranha does not yet cross-compile for .NET 10 (architecture-spine has the full stack table and the .NET 8 EOL 2026-11-10 risk note).
- (constraint) Site B is prioritized ahead of Site A; Site A proceeds in parallel where capacity allows but yields to Site B whenever the two compete for build time, because only Site B carries a fixed external deadline (Tet Am Lich 2027).
- (constraint) Cross-site linking (CAP-5) must stay editorial/contextual only, never a sitewide or reciprocal link block — common ownership of both domains is WHOIS/hosting-discoverable and heavy reciprocal linking between commonly-owned domains is a Google link-scheme/PBN risk pattern.
- (constraint) Site A's installation-required products (e.g. du che nang) are serviceable only Mien Bac through Thanh Hoa; shippable products on both sites serve nationwide.
- (constraint) Both sites are Vietnamese-only, domestic-market only — no i18n/multi-language on either site.
