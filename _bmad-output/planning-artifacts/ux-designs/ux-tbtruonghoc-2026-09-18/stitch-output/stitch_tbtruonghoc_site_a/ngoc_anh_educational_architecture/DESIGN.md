## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-level B2B enterprise aesthetic tailored for school procurement directors, international kindergarten investors, institutional administrators, and educational facility planners. The visual tone balances industrial-scale manufacturing stability with the clean, bright optimism of contemporary educational infrastructure.

### Design Movement: Modern Institutional Corporate
The design movement is strictly clean corporate modernity combined with tactile, architectural clarity:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, institution-ready, dependable, and systematically structured. It repudiates fast-retail gimmicks, aggressive promotional stickers, visual noise, and dark artisanal textures.
- **Atmosphere:** Expansive, luminous, well-aerated with cool-tinted whites and deep academic teals, invoking European standard laboratory and early-childhood institutional environments.
- **Visual Stance:** Clean structural card frames, crisp geometry, and distinct chromatic division of responsibilities (Deep Teal for authority and structure; Warm Amber for purposeful commercial action).

## Colors

The color palette is deliberately calibrated for high legibility, professional rigor, and strategic visual hierarchy:

### Core Color Roles
- **Primary / Brand Teal (`#0E7A6C`):** Represents institutional authority, stability, and trust. Applied to top-level navigation bars, hero backdrops, footers, major section headings, foundational icons, and structural indicators.
- **Secondary / Deep Teal (`#095349`):** Grounded anchor tone used for hover states on primary navigation, high-tier emphasis, and institutional trust seals.
- **Tertiary / Action Amber (`#E3A63B`):** Reserved exclusively for high-priority commercial actions: "Yêu cầu báo giá", "Tư vấn dự án", "Tải hồ sơ năng lực", and key order actions.
- **CTA Typography (`#2B1B02`):** Dark rich umber engineered specifically to ensure AA/AAA accessibility contrast when rendered atop the `#E3A63B` amber surface.

### Surfaces and Neutrals
- **Background / Canvas (`#FCFEFD`):** Crisp, refreshing off-white with a subtle cool mint cast that prevents screen fatigue.
- **Sub-block / Functional Surface (`#EFF8F6`):** Cool light teal tint used for quotation calculators, specification sheets, project phases, and secondary card containers.
- **Main Text (`#123832`):** Deep botanical slate-teal, replacing pure black (`#000000`) to sustain organic warmth and corporate sophistication.
- **Muted Text (`#4A6B65`):** Secondary slate-teal for metadata, serial numbers, SKU designations, and helper strings.
- **Dividers & Borders (`#E3ECE9`):** Subdued architectural boundary line for cards, tables, and section separators.
- **Sub-block Border (`#BFE3DA`):** Deliberate framing tone for `#EFF8F6` callout boxes and technical tags.

### Status & Feedback Tokens
- **Warning / Protocol Note Surface:** `#FFF7E8`
- **Warning Border:** `#F0D7A0`
- **Warning Typography:** `#6B4E10`

### Negative Constraints
- Absolute ban on retail clearance reds, flash-sale ribbons, and bargain-discount tags.
- Absolute ban on warm sepia/wood-grain craft palettes to avoid looking like a boutique wood workshop.

## Typography

The typographic system relies solely on **Mulish**, chosen for its balanced proportions, clean modern geometry, and robust Vietnamese diacritics support.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Headings (Display, H1–H3):** Rendered in weights `700` and `800` with subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.025em`) to confer solidity, industrial scale, and institutional confidence.
- **Body Content:** Rendered at weight `400` with an expansive line height of `1.6` (e.g., 15px font on 24px line-height). Paragraphs must maintain strict optical breathing room to support dense technical specification sheets and regulatory compliance documents.
- **Serif Prohibition:** Serif typefaces are strictly prohibited across all touchpoints to preserve the contemporary engineering posture.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural grid mapped to a 12-column responsive layout engine.

### Grid Foundations
- **Desktop (1200px and up):** 12-column grid, max-width container set to `1280px`, outer margin `2.5rem` (`40px`), column gutter `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, dynamic outer margin `1.5rem` (`24px`), column gutter `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column grid, outer margin `1rem` (`16px`), column gutter `1rem` (`16px`).

### Spacing Strategy
Density should reflect a high-level B2B portal: comfortable without feeling sparse. Data tables and catalog matrices utilize tight padding (`space-xs` and `space-sm`), while promotional showcase blocks and capability introductions utilize expansive sectional buffers (`space-xl` to `4rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows and decorative dimensional effects, relying instead on clean surface segregation and architectural line-work.

### Depth Methodology
- **Standard Hierarchy (Level 0 - Default):** Card components and visual containers sit directly on `#FCFEFD` or `#EFF8F6`, articulated solely through a `1px` border of `#E3ECE9` or `#BFE3DA`.
- **Card Interactive State (Level 1):** Hovering over interactive product cards transitions the border from `#E3ECE9` to `#0E7A6C` with zero offset expansion, accompanied by an extremely faint ambient cast: `0 4px 16px rgba(14, 122, 108, 0.06)`.
- **Overlays and Floating Containers (Level 2):** Flyout mega-menus, quote configuration drawers, and modal windows employ clean ambient elevation: `0 12px 32px rgba(18, 56, 50, 0.08)`, framed cleanly with a 1px border of `#E3ECE9`.
- **Glassmorphism Prohibition:** Frosted glass, chromatic blurs, and skeuomorphic bevels are strictly forbidden.

## Shapes

The design system employs a measured, controlled curvature scale to convey safety without compromising corporate stature.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Micro / Inputs & Interactive Controls:** `6px` to `8px`. Used on buttons, form text inputs, select dropdowns, and search bars.
- **Macro / Structural Cards & Containers:** `10px` to `12px`. Used on product cards, specification modules, case-study panels, and informational callouts.
- **Disallowed Shapes:**
  - `0px` sharp corners (too cold and aggressive for early-childhood infrastructure).
  - Full pill badges (`9999px` borders) on standard interactive buttons (diminishes institutional authority).

## Components

### 1. Primary Action Button (B2B Commercial Focus)
- **Background:** `#E3A63B` (Amber)
- **Text Color:** `#2B1B02` (Dark Umber, Mulish Bold 700)
- **Border:** None
- **Border Radius:** `8px`
- **Padding:** Desktop `12px 24px`, Mobile `10px 20px`
- **States:** Hover transforms surface to `#EDB34D`; Active to `#D49529`. Focus ring renders with `2px` offset in `#0E7A6C`.
- **Use Case:** "Yêu cầu báo giá dự án", "Gửi hồ sơ thầu", "Tải Catalog toàn phần".

### 2. Secondary Button (Institutional Standard)
- **Background:** `transparent`
- **Text Color:** `#0E7A6C` (Mulish Bold 700)
- **Border:** `1.5px solid #0E7A6C`
- **Border Radius:** `8px`
- **States:** Hover renders background `#EFF8F6`; Active transitions background to `#E0F2EE`.
- **Use Case:** "Xem chi tiết thông số kỹ thuật", "Xem tiêu chuẩn an toàn ISO/EN1176".

### 3. Product & Solution Cards
- **Background:** `#FCFEFD` (White Canvas)
- **Border:** `1px solid #E3ECE9`
- **Border Radius:** `12px`
- **Content Organization:** 
  - Image block (16:10 ratio, clean neutral studio lighting, no sticker badges).
  - Institutional Meta: Standard Code (e.g., "TCVN 6238:2017"), Target Age Band.
  - Heading: H3 Mulish 700 `#123832`.
  - Secondary attributes: Raw material specifications (e.g., "Gỗ Dẻ Gai nhập khẩu EU / Thép mạ kẽm sơn tĩnh điện").
  - Action row: Quick quotation toggle or technical drawing download.

### 4. Specification & Quote Sub-blocks
- **Background:** `#EFF8F6`
- **Border:** `1px solid #BFE3DA`
- **Border Radius:** `10px`
- **Padding:** `20px`
- **Role:** Houses bulk pricing tiers, warranty disclaimers, manufacturing lead times, and project-based shipping terms.

### 5. Badges & Compliance Tags

- **Border Radius:** `4px`

- **Content:** Institutional tags only (e.g., "TIÊU CHUẨN MẦM NON QUỐC TẾ", "BẢO HÀNH 36 THÁNG", "CHỨNG NHẬN CO-CQ").

### 7. Advisory & Caution Banners

- **Border:** `1px solid #F0D7A0`

- **Border Radius:** `8px`
